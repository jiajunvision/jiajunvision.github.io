About me

I am Jiajun Liang, a researcher based in Beijing, originally from Zhongshan, Guangdong.
I received my Master of Science in Computer Science from Tsinghua University in 2017 and my Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2014.
My research spans multimodal perception, generation, and embodied intelligence.
